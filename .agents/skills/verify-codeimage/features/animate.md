# Animate

Play one tab morphing into the next, and edit the timing on a strip timeline saved with the document.

## Sub-features

Animate toggle and island in Inspector, Dock and Studio; steps seeded from the tabs on the first open; play, pause, stop and scrub; hold and transition segments with drag resize, arrow nudge and a typed duration; easing presets and a custom curve per transition, Apply to all; header mode (Current tab, Title only with a title, All tabs); Steps menu (Add to animation, Duplicate as next step, Move earlier, Move later, Remove step); preview pill that returns to the code of step N; persistence in IndexedDB for a guest.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Guest `/`, with two tabs or more to have something to play. Press Animate in the frame actions under the canvas (Inspector, Studio, Dock desktop), the play icon after More in Dock's phone bar, or the play icon in Studio's phone actions. The command menu also lists Animate, Play or pause the animation and one command per header mode. The island docks under the canvas. Editable saved projects use the same unified editor; read-only pages hide editing controls.

## Driving it with Playwright

Audit `animate` selects the Editor window (named Add tab), writes two tabs, clicks `[data-animate-toggle]` and waits for `[data-animate-panel]` (region `Animation`) with `aria-pressed="true"` on the toggle. Segments are `[data-animation-segment]` with `data-kind="hold"` or `"transition"`, `data-step-id`, `aria-valuenow` in ms, and `data-selected` / `data-current` as present-or-absent attributes. It clicks button `Play animation`, waits for `[data-animation-stage]:visible` and `[data-preview-pill]`, waits for `[data-animation-time]` to leave `0.00 s`, then clicks the pill and asserts the stage is gone and the editor holds focus. It drags the first hold's `[data-segment-resize]` with real mouse moves, asserts mid-drag that `[data-animation-track]` keeps its `data-axis-ms` while the segment's `aria-valuenow` moved to a multiple of 100, releases, asserts the axis grew by the same amount, then reloads, reopens Animate and reads the same value back.

Other proven handles: slider `Playhead` (click or drag to scrub, release keeps the position); buttons `Pause animation`, `Stop and edit the code`, `Close animation`, `Steps`, `Apply to all`; the header select is the button named `Header …` with options `Current tab`, `Title only`, `All tabs`, and textbox `Title` (the header field is rendered twice, for desktop and phone, so filter on the visible one); `#animationDuration-input` for the selected segment; `[data-easing-trigger]` opens dialog `Easing` with tabs Smooth, Snappy, Strong, Linear, Custom, image `Easing curve`, `[data-easing-dot]` while the playhead is inside that transition, and button `Play transition`. Each scene is a `[data-animation-scene]`; only the shown one is not `display: none`, and its header tabs carry `data-active`. Count saves by wrapping `IDBObjectStore.prototype.put` in an init script and filtering key `document`; the saved shape is `document.animation` in the `keyval-store` database.

Source: `components/Animation/` (`AnimatePanel.tsx`, `TransportBar.tsx`, `StripTimeline.tsx`, `createSegmentResize.ts`, `SegmentDetails.tsx`, `EasingField.tsx`, `EasingCurve.tsx`, `HeaderField.tsx`, `StepsMenu.tsx`, `AnimationPreview.tsx`, `PreviewPill.tsx`, `planPreview.tsx`, `createPreviewSnapshot.ts`), `state/editor/animation.ts`, `state/editor/animationSettings.ts`, `core/animation/easing.ts`, `core/animation/timeline.ts`, `pages/UnifiedEditor/stage.tsx`, `pages/UnifiedEditor/variants/*/index.tsx`, `i18n/animation.ts`.

## Gotchas

Drive it through the controls. `scripts/parity/live-animate.ts` seeds fixtures through the store. Its regression lanes use the visible controls for step removal, playback and commands, keyboard details, custom easing commits, and long-strip scrolling. Older Header/Pill/Scrub cases still seek through the store; audit covers opening and playback from the user entry points.

A click that succeeds does not prove a control is visible. Playwright scrolls `overflow: hidden` boxes, and the kit popover clips its content, so compare each control's box with the dialog's box and the viewport, and hit-test their centers and edges before clicking them. The Easing lane checks the curve, Custom field, validation message and Play transition. Check that the curve, the Custom field and Play transition sit inside dialog `Easing` in every layout and at 390×844.

Assert displayed values, not only stored ones: after selecting a segment, `#animationDuration-input` must show its duration, including the literal `0` for a zero-duration hold before focus and after blur. Type into it with the keyboard and commit with Enter or Tab. The duration uses a string-valued kit TextField so zero remains visible, with stable kit step buttons. Enter preserves focus. The field commits on Enter, blur, Arrow Up/Down and the duration steppers. Escape reverts its pending value and retains the selection and input focus. The steppers are pointer controls; Arrow Up/Down provide the keyboard path, and Tab skips the step buttons. Custom easing also commits on blur; entering a preset’s points keeps the Custom field mounted. A custom curve's y values are stored clamped to 0..1, and an x outside 0..1 shows `Use four numbers with x between 0 and 1.` and saves nothing.

Steps join only on the first open. A tab added later needs Steps → Add to animation. With nothing selected, Duplicate, Move and Remove are disabled. A menu takes up to 0.8 s to leave the page after it closes, so wait for `[role=menu]` to detach before the next action. Duplicate as next step returns focus to the new tab's code after the menu's focus restoration; other menu actions return to Steps. Closing a tab removes its steps, and with one step left `Play animation` is disabled. Preview planning pairs settings with their matching measurement snapshot; timing-only edits reuse the measurement. RemoveDuringPreview removes each of three steps while paused and playing, checks menu closure and the remaining strip, and waits for IndexedDB to contain the removal. Fail on any `pageerror` or nonexistent-droppable warning.

Typed keys reach the code only while the preview stage holds focus. Play, Play transition and the playback command move focus to the stage, so printable keys edit the current step and bypass app hotkeys. PlayFocus checks both the Play button and command menu. Clicking the stage focuses it; only the pill, Enter, Esc or a printable key returns to the code.

In the default macOS window the add button has no accessible name and every `[data-host-index]` tab exists twice (canvas and export copy). A save is debounced: wait for the write before reloading. The What's new nudge of a first visit covers the toggle's left edge in Inspector desktop and Dock phone. The strip has one tab stop. Up/Down, Home/End move selection; Left/Right nudge its duration (Alt uses 10 ms). Tab reaches that segment’s duration, then Easing and Apply to all. Tab from the duration reaches Easing directly, and Shift+Tab returns to the duration. Apply to all retains focus when every transition matches, announces `aria-disabled`, and ignores further activations. Long strips use one geometry table for segment widths, 3 px gaps, ruler ticks, the playhead, scrubbing and resizing. Zero-duration holds retain at least 8 px. On phones, each segment reserves 80 px so its selection/scrolling area and resize target each have 40 px. Gaps and zero-duration slots map to their boundary time; time-to-position chooses the following nonzero segment at a shared boundary. Dragging freezes the table's time scale until release. Playback and new selections reveal their position; wheel and touch scrolling suspend playback following for 1.5 seconds, and resizing suspends it throughout the drag. An explicit selection is revealed even just after a manual scroll. Controls checks 40 px tall phone buttons and Settings segmented-control Escape behavior; the Settings dialog can be an `alertdialog`, so match both dialog roles. The Animate command focuses its panel or toggle. The app theme follows `localStorage` `@store/ui` (`themeMode`), not the browser's colour scheme, so set it there for light-theme proof. Audit `animate` covers opening, playback, the pill and resize persistence in Inspector desktop. `animate-focus` and `animate-geometry` cover the review regressions in all three layouts at 1440×900 and 390×844. Easing presets, custom curves and header modes still need their specialist lanes.

## MP4 export

After two steps are saved, Export video and its arrow open MP4 settings while Animate is open. Export as image returns to the image controls. The dialog owns the 24/30/50/60 fps choice and persists it in the animation settings, so reopening the dialog and later exports use the same rate. The Animate panel still has no fps field. Export measures the same static step layouts and uses planPreviewTimeline, frameRules and the same header/timing/easing settings as this preview. A pure core/animation/target-layout.ts helper places windows in social targets.

The five `animation-mp4` audit cases cover layouts, failures, compositing, export modes and static-frame parity; see [Export](export.md) for controls, worker cancellation, unsupported configurations and remaining platform checks.
