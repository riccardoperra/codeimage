Adds an Animate panel to the editor. With two or more tabs, it plays the morph from one tab to the next in place of the editor frame, with the same keyframes the export renders. Steps follow the tabs and can be reordered, and each step's hold, the transition, the easing, and the frame rate are saved with the project.
