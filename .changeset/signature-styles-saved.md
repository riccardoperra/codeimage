Give the new editor's signature three styles (Minimal, Pill and Card), an optional platform icon, a Card avatar, three positions and ink that follows the background, all sized to the frame so they stay readable on social exports. Load the Card avatar from a public GitHub, Bluesky or Mastodon profile, or from an upload. Save up to three signatures per user, in the account or, for guests, in the browser, with one default for new snippets. Clicking the signature on the canvas opens its settings.
